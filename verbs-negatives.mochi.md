-------
What is the rule for negative -る verbs?
---
Drop the -る and add -ない
-------
What is the rule for negative -う verbs?
---
Replace the -う with the -あ equivalent and add -ない
-------
What is the rule for negative -う verbs that end in -う?
---
Replace the -う with the わ and add -ない
